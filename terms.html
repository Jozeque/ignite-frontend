2. What Stride Is · System Requirements

Stride is a sound design engine. It ships as two editions that share the same visual canvas: Stride VST — a VST3 plugin that hosts your instruments and effects and modulates their parameters live in any VST3 host (Cubase, Studio One, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig, Ableton, and more), and which, in Ableton Live running StrideBridge, can also map supported Ableton Live devices — and StrideLink — a Max for Live device that modulates your racks and native devices inside Ableton Live and injects the motion into your clips.

Check the requirements for the edition you'll use before purchase — it is your responsibility to verify your setup:

Stride VST requires any VST3-compatible host on macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later, or Windows 10/11. It does NOT run in Logic Pro (Audio Units only) or Pro Tools (AAX only).

StrideBridge is included with Stride VST and is optional. It requires Ableton Live 11 or later with Max for Live (Live Suite, or Live Standard plus the Max for Live add-on). Without StrideBridge, Stride VST modulates only the plugins you host inside it.

StrideLink requires:

  • Ableton Live 11 Suite or higher — OR Ableton Live 11 Standard plus the Max for Live add-on. StrideLink does NOT work with Ableton Live Lite, Intro, or versions older than 11.
  • Python 3 installed on your computer (pre-installed on macOS; Windows users can install it from python.org).
  • macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later, or Windows 10/11.

An internet connection is required for initial license activation of either edition.

Neither edition is a synth or a DAW, and neither generates audio on its own. Stride VST modulates the VST3 plugins you host inside it and, in Ableton Live running StrideBridge, supported Ableton Live devices in your set; StrideLink modulates devices in your Ableton set and writes the motion into your clips. Stride does not replace your DAW.
