📖 The playbook (my read + sequencing — click to collapse)
The diagnosis

A week of zero sales right after the flat-$59 flip tells me this is a conversion problem, not a traffic problem. Leads exist; they aren't completing. The biggest, cheapest leak is abandoned carts — people who opened checkout and bounced. Their intent is already proven. We start there before we spend a dollar on new traffic.

Sequencing — three sprints

1 · Stop the leak (week 1). Abandoned-cart email + WhatsApp concierge to every high-intent lead. Ask the one question: "what stopped you?" Diagnose before discounting.

2 · Warm the pool (weeks 2–3). The 🎁 free-pack list is the largest asset — they opted in but came for samples. Nurture from "here are sounds" → "here's the engine that makes infinite ones from racks you already own," then a bonus/bundle bridge (REKZ coupon).

3 · Proof flywheel (weeks 3–4). Free access to 3–5 engaged users → testimonials/UGC → relaunch the 2.0 "Inject to Clip" story to all cold/new leads with social proof behind it.

Messaging angles by segment

Abandoned cart → objection handling + a 60-sec inject demo. Human 1:1, not a broadcast. The reasons are usually: Live-version compatibility, "is this AI?", or value doubt at $59.

Free-pack → teach first, sell second. The pack is the hook; Stride is the upgrade that makes endless versions from their own gear.

New / waitlist → lead with the 2.0 one-click direct-inject — the product story changed, give them a reason to act now.

Churned → mostly intel. Learn the refund reason; a light "we shipped 2.0" win-back is secondary.

Guardrails (brand rules)
  • Never mention AI / "generates music for you." It's a sound-design engine you drive.
  • No founding-member / scarcity framing — that era ended. Use bonuses, bundles, and concierge, not fake urgency.
  • Producer voice — racks, clips, automation, Live. Confidence, no hype.
  • Diagnose the abandoned-cart reason before discounting the whole list.
