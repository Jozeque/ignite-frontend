Getting Started

Modulate everything in your session. 5 simple steps.

Everything runs inside Stride

No setup, no install. Load your synth and effects right here, draw motion, and Stride modulates them live in any DAW, locked to the transport. Your whole rack and its curves save with the project.

The Workflow

1

Add your synth

Hit + Add and pick an instrument (VST3, or AU on Mac). It loads inside Stride and lands in your favorites for next time. Add effects after it the same way and they chain in series, so Stride can modulate the whole rack.

2

Map the knobs you want to move

Press Map (it lights up), then click any knob in the synth or its effects. Each one drops in as a lane automatically. Press Map again when you're done. Your curves modulate the moment you draw them, playing or not.

3

Draw the motion

Draw a curve on any lane, or fire a Motion tool (Chaos, Neuro, Reflector, S&H, Prism, Bloom) to lay motion across every lane at once. Reshape, Mutate, Mirror, until it feels right.

4

Set the loop length

Pick Bars (4, 8, 16, 32). That sets how long the modulation loop runs before it repeats.

5

Press play

Hit play in your DAW. Stride modulates the synth live, in sync with the transport. Tweak a curve and it updates as it plays.

Want more params? Just map them.

Press Map again and click more knobs, on the synth or any effect in the chain. They auto-appear as new lanes. Draw, and they modulate with the rest.

Long chain? Open one device at a time

Every loaded device shows in the bar under the logo. Click the ⛶ on a device to open just that plugin's window, or the ⛶ in the control bar to open them all. The ✕ removes one, and Ctrl+Z brings it back.

StrideLink installed

One step left before you can inject

In Ableton, open Settings → Link, Tempo & MIDI, find Control Surface, and choose StrideInject. This is what lets Stride write automation into your clips.

Restart Ableton if it was already open — it only detects StrideInject on launch.

Install to Ableton

One-time setup

Stride needs to drop its Max device (StrideLink) into your Ableton User Library. Afterwards, open Ableton's browser → User Library → Stride → drag StrideLink onto any MIDI track.

Tip: Not sure where your User Library is? In Ableton, right-click User Library in the browser sidebar → Show in Explorer. That's the folder Stride writes to.
