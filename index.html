Inside the Engine

Uncompromising Tools for Professional Output

Sound Design (.ALC)

Go beyond notes. Stride compiles your generated MIDI directly into an Ableton Live Clip (.alc), injecting chaotic, dynamic synth automations (LFOs, Drunk Walks) mathematically synced to the groove.

Cosmic Mode

Ancient math meets modern production. Generate cinematic, hypnotic sequences using strict rhythmic algorithms (Tesla 3-6-9, Fibonacci) paired with exotic microtonal scales like Hirajōshi and Byzantine.

Studio Assistant

Talk to the engine naturally. Describe your vibe, genre, and emotional intent. The system automatically determines the perfect BPM, Key, and Style, translating your words into professional MIDI arrangements.

"A melancholic 85 BPM Neo-Soul chord progression. Use lush 9ths and 11ths, keep it sparse..."

The Seed Engine

Drop your MIDI sketch. We'll build the track around it. The engine analyzes your original timing and harmony to generate perfectly synced Basslines, Counter-Melodies, or Pads without ever overwriting your original notes.
