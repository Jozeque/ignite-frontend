Tendril × Stride

A pattern in Tendril. A click in Stride. A new variation every time, and you keep what works.

Three engines in one instrument.

OSC 1, OSC 2 and OSC 3 are three complete voices, each on its own tab: its own sound, its own effects and its own eight motion lanes. No fixed roles: each one becomes whatever sound you design, and the three play off each other.

64

Partials per hit

Draw the waveform with ten knobs: lean it, thin it, stretch it from wood toward metal, carve a region out.

8

Motion lanes per engine

Give any knob a lane and the drawing keeps moving while the beat plays, in sync with the song or free in Hz.

3

Patterns at once

One row of steps per engine, each with its own length, rate and swing, so the three drift against each other.

OSC FX, filters, drive

Shape it before it ever hits a filter.

SPECTRUM and SHAPE OSC FX fold and sieve the waveform at the source. Then BODY, DRIVE and FILTER, and GLITCH and SHARDS to break it into repeats and echo bursts.

The sequencer

A sequencer that plays all three.

Each engine gets its own row: velocity, chance, ratchets and notes per step, eight patterns deep. Three lengths running against each other turn one bar into a phrase. Take the beat into your DAW as a MIDI clip when it lands.

The mixer

One MIDI track plays the whole kit.

A fader, pan, mute and solo for each engine, a key zone so every note finds its engine, and a ceiling on the master. The mixer has motion lanes of its own.
