Tendril × Stride

A pattern in Tendril. A click in Stride. Fresh motion on what you mapped, and you keep what works.

Shape the sound. Keep it moving.

Ten knobs reshape the harmonics at the source, and every one can move. Each of the three engines has its own sound, effects and sequencer row, so tone and rhythm keep evolving together.

64

Partials per note

Lean the spectrum, thin it, stretch it from wood toward metal, carve a region out.

8

Motion lanes per engine

Give any knob a lane and the sound keeps moving while it plays, in sync with the song or free in Hz.

3

Patterns at once

One row of steps per engine, each with its own length, rate and swing, so the three drift against each other.

OSC FX, filters, drive

Bend it before it ever hits a filter.

SPECTRUM and SHAPE OSC FX fold and sieve the waveform at the source. Then BODY, DRIVE and FILTER, and GLITCH and SHARDS to break it into repeats and echo bursts.

The sequencer

A sequencer that plays all three.

Each engine gets its own row: velocity, chance, ratchets and notes per step, eight patterns deep. Three lengths running against each other turn one bar into a phrase. Take the sequence into your DAW as a MIDI clip when it lands.

The mixer

Three engines. One playable instrument.

A fader, pan, mute and solo for each engine, a key zone so every note finds its engine, and a ceiling on the master. The mixer has motion lanes of its own.
