This is not a Stride tutorial

The goal is not to show you where the buttons are.

It is to get you into the mindset Stride was built around:

Explore more. Commit less. Follow what sounds interesting. Keep what works. Reroll the rest.

Instead of deciding exactly where a sound should go before you start, we create possibilities, listen, react and let the best direction reveal itself.

What we do in the hour

Start with your own setup

Your DAW. Your plugins. Your sounds.

We choose something you already know well and start pushing it beyond the directions you would normally reach.

Explore instead of over-planning

We map meaningful parameters, create movement, try variations, lock what hits, reroll what does not, reshape, resample and keep following the interesting accidents.

You will see how to move quickly between possibilities without losing control of the sound.

Learn what to listen for

The important part is not clicking for more variations. It is knowing what deserves to stay.

We will break down how to recognize interesting movement, when to lock something, when to keep exploring, and when a direction is worth committing to.

Take the workflow with you

You keep every chain and sound we build.

More importantly, you leave with a process you can bring into every future sound-design session:
