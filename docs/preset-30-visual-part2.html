Presets 06-30

Continuation. Full reactive visualizations: root + secondary + ghost lanes.

06Ricochet

Call and response. Sharp stabs (bars 1+3) alternate with fluid rolls (bars 2+4).

Call (1+3): 3 sharp stabs + silence. Drive spikes on stabs only. No reverb — dry and punchy.
Response (2+4): Fluid arc. Reverb blooms. Delay fills the space. Ghost lanes split: odd=call, even=response.
07Wobble

Variable-rate sine. Speed changes: slow → fast → peak → decelerate. FM-style.

FM-style: Sine frequency: bar1 ~1Hz, bar2 ~2Hz, bar3 ~4Hz (peak), bar4 decelerates to ~1.5Hz. The RATE itself is an evolving curve.
Reactive: Res HIGH when slow (audible wobble). Drive HIGH when fast (aggression). Ghost = phase-cascaded wobble → massive stereo.
08Stagger

Asymmetric hit patterns: short-short-long-short. Changes each bar. No two bars the same.

Patterns: Bar1: S-S-L-S. Bar2: L-S-S-L. Bar3: S-L-S-REST. Bar4: irregular 6 hits. Drive spikes on short hits.
Reactive: Filter sweeps during long holds (fills the sustain). Ghost lanes randomize which hits they accent — no two lanes emphasize the same hit.
09Pendulum

Damped spring. One pluck — rings out losing energy. Real harmonic decay physics.

Physics: One "pluck" at beat 0. Oscillation rings out — each cycle smaller. By bar 4, nearly still. Real damped harmonic motion (amplitude decays exponentially).
Reactive: Reverb grows as spring dies (fills the void). Drive fades with amplitude. Ghost: same spring, different damping factors — some ring longer.
10Fracture

Clean groove that breaks apart. Fractal self-similar degradation.

Fractal: Bar 1: clean 4/4. Bar 2: beat 3 splits. Bar 3: gaps + scramble. Bar 4: shards with silence. Each split contains smaller splits.
Reactive: Reverb grows (fills increasing silence). Drive harshens remaining shards. Ghost lanes fragment at different rates — dissolving effect.

Pumper Variants

11The Pumper — Tight

The reference. Tight → syncopated → open → double-time. All lanes react per bar.

4 phases: Tight 4/4 → syncopated → open sweep → double-time build. Resonance: counter→wobble→zero.
Reactive: Delay fills gaps bar2-3, cuts bar4. Ghost lanes: tight micro bar1, wider bar2-3, dense cascade bar4.
12The Pumper — Half-Time

2-bar pump cycle. Hit + long exponential decay. Resonance + delay fill the tail.

Shape: Sharp attack, long exponential tail over 2 bars. Resonance peaks during tail — fills the decay.
Reactive: Delay blooms in bars 2+4 (tail zone). Ghost lanes follow pump energy at micro level. Spacious, heavy, hypnotic.
13The Pumper — Reverse

Values rise BEFORE the beat, snap down ON the beat. Sucking-in energy.

Reverse: Exponential ramp UP before each beat, instant snap DOWN on the beat. Energy flows backward — "sucking" instead of "pumping".
Reactive: Resonance counter-rises before each snap (tension building). Drive spikes at snap moment. Ghost lanes carry the tension buildup at micro level.
14The Pumper — Triplet

3-against-4 polyrhythm. Triplets resolve to straight in bars 3-4. The lock-in is the climax.

Polyrhythm: Bars 1-2: 3 pumps/bar (triplet). Bars 3-4: straight 4/bar. The moment it locks to grid = maximum groove satisfaction.
Reactive: Filter wider in triplet zone (more space for "wrong" feel). Resonance peaks at resolution. Ghost tracks rhythmic tension.
15The Pumper — Shark Fin

Asymmetric: exponential rise + logarithmic fall. Each bar a different attack/release ratio.

Shark Fin: Bar 1: sharp attack, slow decay. Bar 2: slow rise, sharp drop. Bar 3: alternates within bar. Bar 4: mixed ratios. Asymmetric = natural.
Reactive: Res uses INVERSE asymmetry — when root has sharp attack, res has slow attack. Drive spikes on sharp edges only. Ghost carries the asymmetry at micro level.
16The Pumper — Decay

Same attack, 4 different decay curves: linear, convex, concave, exponential.

Same rhythm, 4 textures: Linear=robotic. Convex=holds energy. Concave=snappy. Exponential=natural acoustic decay. Each bar is a different character.
Reactive: Resonance uses INVERSE decay type — when root is convex, res is concave. Natural complementary. Drive follows root shape at 50%.

Sweep & Evolve

17Slow Burn

Glacial WT sweep. Filter fills the silence, yields when WT takes over.

Trade-off: Filter does sweeps while WT barely moves (bars 1-2). Yields when WT takes over (bars 3-4). Reverb counter-tracks WT.
Reactive: Chorus rate picks up as WT enters bright zone. Ghost lanes: calm bars 1-2, increasingly agitated bars 3-4 (tracking energy of the WT zone).
18Deep Breath

Inhale/exhale. Reverb blooms on inhale. Drive compresses on exhale. Different lane owners per phase.

Phase ownership: Reverb ONLY on inhale — space opens. Drive ONLY on exhale — compression. Width follows breath shape.
Reactive: Each lane "owns" a different breath phase. Some only exist at peak. Some only during rest. Ghost lanes expand on inhale, contract on exhale.
19Tide

Two sweeps in opposite directions crossing at bar 2. The intersection is where everything activates.

Crossing: Sweep UP + Sweep DOWN cross at bar 2. Resonance and delay peak at the intersection.
Reactive: Ghost lanes' activity = inverse of distance between roots. Maximum movement at crossing. Calm at the edges (bar 1, bar 4). Beautiful tension arc.
20Orbit

Two sines at golden ratio (1:1.618). Where they ALIGN = explosion. Where they CANCEL = gates slam shut.

Convergence = explosion: When both sines align, drive goes EXTREME, all ghost lanes activate, maximum energy. The convergence at bar 3 is the biggest — both roots peak together.
Cancellation = void: When sines oppose, reverb fills the silence, gates close, ghost lanes die. The contrast between convergence and cancellation IS the groove. Irrational ratio = never repeats.
21Whip

Sharp crack accents with long whip-tail decays. Evolving crack positions + reactive reverb tails.

Whip: Sharp instant crack → long exponential tail. Each crack has a different tail length. Crack positions shift across bars — groove walks like Strut but with impact.
Reactive: Reverb catches each crack (bloom intensity matches crack intensity). Drive spikes on crack only. Ghost builds anticipation before each crack.

Build & Surge

22Surge

Staircase build. Fake drop at 75%. Real drop at end. Cascading release.

Fake drop at 75%: Reverb goes HUGE (rings out). Width collapses to mono. Everything resets.
Real drop: Reverb snaps DRY (total contrast). Drive holds for impact. Ghost density peaks before each drop.
23Ignite

Exponential build. Lanes join at different ignition points — engine starting.

y = x^4: Barely moves bars 1-2. Fast bar 3. Vertical bar 4. Most action in the last 25% of the timeline.
Cascade ignition: Lane A joins bar 2. Lane B joins bar 3. Lane C joins bar 4. Engine starting — cylinders fire one by one.
24Collapse

Full intensity → glitching → fragmenting → shards. Not a fade — a structural failure. Things BREAK.

Structural failure: Bar 1: full solid intensity. Bar 2: starts glitching — random cuts to zero, resonance goes wild. Bar 3: fragments — mostly silence with stabs. Bar 4: occasional shards in a void.
Reactive: Drive gets harsh and erratic as the structure fails. Reverb fills the growing void. Ghost lanes die at different rates — some survive longer. Not a fade — a CRASH.
25Tension

Escalating chaos. Starts calm, stutter appears, noise grows, wobble accelerates, everything rattles. Never releases.

Escalation: Bar 1: dead calm. Bar 2: slight stutter appears. Bar 3: filter wobble accelerates, resonance starts screaming. Bar 4: everything rattles — wobble at max speed, ghost lanes in full chaos.
Never releases: Ramp reaches 0.98 but never 1.0. The stutter/wobble keeps INCREASING. Drive goes exponential. The brain braces for a drop that doesn't come. Pair before someone else's drop — maximum anticipation.

Texture & Ghost

26Ghost Machine

Interlocking polyrhythmic gates. Every lane has a DIFFERENT step pattern. 16ths + triplets + dotted 8ths overlapping. Evolves across 4 bars.

16th gate Triplet gate Dotted 8th gate Sweep underlayer More gate patterns
Polyrhythmic gates: 16th gate (root) + triplet gate + dotted 8th gate — three different rhythms interlocking. Where they align = PEAK. Where they oppose = silence. Density increases each bar.
NOT subtle: These are full 0-to-1 gates, not micro-drift. Sweep underlayer changes which gate pattern dominates. GateKeeper-style precision with multi-lane polyrhythmic complexity no single plugin can do.
27Shimmer

Cascading 32nd staccato bursts. Rapid sparkling fire that ripples across lanes like a waterfall. Density builds.

Burst pattern Cascade echo Reverb tails Ripple lanes
Waterfall: Sparse staccato bursts in bar 1 → denser → bar 4 is a WALL of rapid fire. Cascade echoes follow each burst, offset slightly — creates sparkling ripple effect across lanes.
NOT subtle: Full 0-to-1 staccato gates. Reverb catches burst clusters (blooms between dense sections). Each lane fires at a slightly different time — 100 lanes firing staccato at near-same-time = glittering shimmer wall.
28Murk

Deep slow ducking pressure waves. Heavy GateKeeper-style gates with long exponential tails. Underwater pressure.

Deep pressure gates Counter-fill (active in voids) Sub rumble Overlapping tails
Deep pressure: Massive slow gates — snap to full intensity then long exponential decay over 2+ beats. Only 3 gates across 4 bars. Each one is an EVENT. Counter-fill blooms in the voids between gates.
Underwater: Sub rumble oscillation underneath everything. Ghost lanes = same deep gates but OFFSET, creating overlapping pressure waves. Like being underwater and feeling the bass through your body. NOT subtle — heavy, powerful, slow.

Cascade & Special

29Cascade

One sine ripples across 100 lanes. First lanes hit hard, last lanes whisper. Wave mutates as it travels.

Ripple: Root sine → each lane offset by 1/N, amplitude decreasing. First 5 lanes strong. Next 10 medium. Last 85 whisper-level. The wave shape softens as it propagates.
Best on: 30+ bandpass filters. The Ableton UI shows every filter moving, all slightly out of phase. THE content piece. This is what sells the tool visually.
30Chaotic Engine

Rules broken on purpose. Chaos zones alternate with clarity. Reverb is the inverse anchor.

Structure: Chaos zones (1+3): everything moves erratically. Drive moves WITH root (rules deliberately broken). Bar 3 is denser/wider than bar 1. Escalating chaos.
Anchor: Reverb is the inverse — dry in chaos, HUGE in clarity. The contrast makes both states hit harder. The alternation IS the groove.
