Recommendation — run it as a small system, with #3 Transform as the hero

For a tool whose whole magic is motion, the best still image is one that implies motion. Transform does that and stays dark/fine/cool, so it satisfies every part of the brief at once. Keep Gallery as the quiet identity post and Statement for reach — three covers, one language. That variety is what makes the grid feel like a feed worth following instead of a shop shelf.

Device-forward — show the gear they own

You're right: the recognizable device is a promo lever, not the problem. What made the old covers feel like a store was the badges / CTA / price and the busy full-bleed collage — not the screenshot. Shown with intent, the device earns relevance ("I own that") and sets up your best hook — make your plugins do things they never do. Here it's a co-star: framed like real hardware, patched into Stride, curves as the through-line. Still cool, still fine — now it promotes.

Where the plugin screenshot goes (when you want it)

The current studio fuses a full-bleed plugin shot into every cover — that's a big part of the "ad" feeling. In this direction the screenshot becomes optional: a small, copper-framed inset (a "now-playing" chip) on demo posts only. The curves + type stay the constant; the plugin is a guest, not the host.

Net: most Reels need no screenshot at all for the cover — the video shows the tool. The cover's job is the idea.
