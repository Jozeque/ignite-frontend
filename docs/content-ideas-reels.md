# Stride — Instagram Reel Content Ideas

Goal: Fill the screen with plugin movement, sound design that hits hard, visually impressive automation. Each reel = 10-20 seconds, before/after or live canvas-to-playback.

---

## 1. "30 Bandpass Filters" (Your Proven Formula)
**Plugin:** KHS Bandpass x30 in a rack
**What:** Chaos all 30 filter params — cutoffs, resonance, gain all moving independently
**Why it works:** Screen full of knobs, the sound goes from static to alive. Chunky, phat, immediate wow
**Hook:** "What happens when you automate 30 filters at once"

## 2. Serum Wavetable Morph Storm
**Plugin:** Serum (or Vital) — both oscillators
**What:** Automate wavetable position, WT pos fine, warp amount, unison detune, filter cutoff across both OSCs. Use Bloom from one slow sine sweep — each param gets a variation
**Why it works:** Serum's visual wavetable display animates in real time. The sound morphs constantly — every beat is a different texture
**Hook:** "One curve. Bloom. Every Serum knob moves differently"

## 3. OTT Breathing Machine
**Plugin:** Ableton OTT (or Xfer OTT) on a pad/chord
**What:** Automate depth, time, and all 3 band amounts with synced pump curves at different rates. Low band = 1 bar pump, mid = 1/2 bar, high = 1/4 bar
**Why it works:** OTT is universal — every producer knows it. The multi-rate pumping creates a breathing, living texture. Looks like the compressor is alive
**Hook:** "OTT but it's breathing at 3 different speeds"

## 4. Reverb Into Black Hole
**Plugin:** Valhalla VintageVerb or Ableton Reverb
**What:** Start dry. Over 8 bars, automate decay → max, size → max, diffusion → max, predelay → 0, dry/wet → 100%. Use slow Bezier curves that accelerate. Then snap everything back to dry on beat 1
**Why it works:** The sound swallows itself. Visually all the reverb knobs slowly open up. The snap-back is satisfying
**Hook:** "Sound design trick: the black hole reverb"

## 5. Filter Rack — 8 Autofilters in Series
**Plugin:** Ableton Auto Filter x8 in a rack, each set to different filter types (LP, HP, BP, Notch, alternating)
**What:** Automate all cutoffs with staggered sine curves (phase offset per filter). Resonance with inverse curves
**Why it works:** 8 filter displays all moving out of phase = mesmerizing visual. The sound is a complex, evolving filter sweep that's impossible to program manually
**Hook:** "8 filters. 8 phases. One rack"

## 6. Granular Destruction
**Plugin:** Ableton Granulator II or any granular synth
**What:** Automate grain size, position, spray, pitch, density all at once. Use Glitch template — random short bursts on every param
**Why it works:** Granular is inherently visual (waveform display moves). The sound goes from recognizable sample to absolute chaos. Perfect for "how did you make that" comments
**Hook:** "Turned a piano into an alien language"

## 7. Saturator Chain Meltdown
**Plugin:** Ableton Saturator x4 (or Decapitator/Saturn)
**What:** Stack 4 saturators, different drive types. Automate drive, tone, dry/wet on all 4 with escalating intensity curves — starts clean, ends destroyed
**Why it works:** Progressive destruction is addictive to watch. The meters slam harder each bar. Sound goes from warm to absolutely wrecked
**Hook:** "4 saturators. 8 bars. Total meltdown"

## 8. Delay Feedback Chaos
**Plugin:** Ableton Echo or Ping Pong Delay
**What:** Automate feedback near self-oscillation, delay time (creates pitch shifting), filter, reverb send. Use freehand curves for organic, unpredictable movement
**Why it works:** Delay feedback near max = unpredictable sonic territory. The display goes crazy. Users will ask "how is this not feeding back?"
**Hook:** "Delay feedback at 99% — controlled chaos"

## 9. Drum Rack Parameter Takeover
**Plugin:** Ableton Drum Rack with 8-16 cells loaded
**What:** Scan the full drum rack. Automate tuning, decay, tone across ALL drum cells simultaneously. Use Pump template — every hit retunes itself
**Why it works:** Every single drum pad's parameters move together. The beat transforms in real time — same pattern, completely different groove every bar. Producers will lose it
**Hook:** "Same drum loop. Every bar sounds different"

## 10. Frequency Shifter Spiral
**Plugin:** Ableton Frequency Shifter x3
**What:** Stack 3 freq shifters on a vocal/pad. Automate coarse + fine frequency on all 3 with slow sine curves at different rates (3:5:7 ratio). Ring mod mode
**Why it works:** Creates metallic, alien harmonics that spiral around each other. The frequency displays create a hypnotic visual pattern
**Hook:** "3 frequency shifters turning a vocal into a synth"

## 11. Corpus Resonance Sweep
**Plugin:** Ableton Corpus (resonance effect)
**What:** Automate frequency, decay, resonance type, listening position on a percussive loop. Slow sweep through the resonant frequencies
**Why it works:** Corpus makes anything melodic. Sweeping through frequencies on a hi-hat loop = instant techno content. The resonance body visual animates beautifully
**Hook:** "Made a hi-hat loop melodic with one plugin"

## 12. The Full Rack Reset
**Plugin:** Any complex instrument rack (Analog, Operator, Wavetable)
**What:** Chaos ALL parameters (20-40+). Let it play for 4 bars of pure sound design madness. Then show the canvas clear → apply → clean preset restored. The contrast sells the tool
**Why it works:** Shows both extremes — total chaos AND precise control. The "before Stride / after Stride" angle
**Hook:** "40 parameters. One click. Sound design done"

## Production Tips for All Reels

- **Screen record Ableton** with the plugin UI visible and maximized — the knob movement IS the content
- **Split screen**: Stride canvas on one half, plugin UI on the other
- **Start with the result** (2 sec of the crazy sound), then show how you made it
- **Keep it under 15 seconds** — the algorithm favors replays
- **Caption formula:** "[number] [thing] + [action] = [result]"
- **Always end on the drop/impact moment** — cut before it gets boring
- **Use trending audio format:** mute original, layer your sound over a trending reel template if it fits
