Preset Bank v1 — 30 Presets

Each preset = 100 reactive lanes. Root curves define the story, everything else derives. Bars: 4 or 8.

Mix: 10 groove/choppy + 6 pumper + 5 sweep/evolve + 4 build/surge + 3 texture + 2 cascade

Groove & Chop 10 presets

01 — Slicer
Choppy gate pattern that evolves. Bar 1: clean 8ths. Bar 2: removes every other hit. Bar 3: triplet feel. Bar 4: rapid stutter.
Root 1: evolving gate pattern Root 2: slow filter sweep underneath
Root 1: gate/chop pattern — 8th notes bar 1, half removed bar 2, triplets bar 3, 32nd stutter bar 4. Values snap between 0 and 0.8-1.0 (not always full).
Root 2: slow filter sweep 0.3→0.7 across 4 bars — changes the TONE of each chop section.
Secondary: resonance counter-tracks filter. Drive spikes on the chop hits only. Delay fills the gaps between chops.
Tertiary: phase-cascaded chop pattern at 10% — adds width and shimmer to each slice.
02 — Bounce
Swung groove. Not straight — everything has 15% swing. Off-beat accents that make static sounds groove.
Root 1: swung pulse with off-beat accents Root 2: drift on detune
Root 1: pulse on 8th notes with 15% swing applied. Every other hit is softer (0.6 vs 1.0). Bar 3 adds ghost notes (0.2 value) on the 16th.
Root 2: gentle detune drift ±0.1 — makes the groove feel human, not quantized.
Secondary: filter follows the accent pattern — opens on strong beats, closes on weak. Pan alternates L/R on the swing.
Tertiary: micro-groove — all lanes have the same swing timing, but at whisper levels.
03 — Pocket
Tight, locked groove with syncopated accents. Bars 1-2 repeat. Bars 3-4 open up with fills.
Root 1: syncopated accent pattern Root 2: sidechain pump (slower, 2-beat cycle)
Root 1: accents on beat 1, and-of-2, beat 4 (syncopated). Bars 1-2 identical. Bar 3: accent on and-of-3 added. Bar 4: extra ghost notes fill in.
Root 2: slower pump — 2-beat cycle instead of 1-beat. Creates a loping, half-time feel underneath the accents.
Secondary: filter opens sharply on each accent then decays. Reverb blooms between the 2-beat pump hits (fills the space).
Tertiary: lock to the 2-beat pump at micro level — everything breathes at the same pace.
04 — Shaker
Hi-hat energy. Fast 16th note movement on top, half-time anchor underneath. Evolving density.
Root 1: 16th note pattern, evolving density Root 2: half-time anchor pulse
Root 1: 16th notes. Bar 1: every other 16th active (sparse). Bar 2: 3 out of 4 active. Bar 3: all active (full). Bar 4: rolls — accelerating 16th→32nd. Values vary 0.4-1.0 (not all full intensity).
Root 2: simple half-time pulse — hits on beat 1 and 3 only. The slow anchor that the fast pattern dances around.
Secondary: filter tracks the 16th density — opens as pattern gets denser. Drive follows the half-time anchor.
Tertiary: the 16th pattern phase-cascaded — each lane starts on a different 16th. Creates stereo shimmer.
05 — Strut
Walking groove. Accent pattern shifts forward by one 8th note each bar — the groove literally moves.
Root 1: shifting accent (moves each bar) Root 2: filter follows the accent
Root 1: strong accent + 2 ghost notes pattern. Bar 1: accent on beat 1. Bar 2: accent shifts to and-of-1. Bar 3: accent on beat 2. Bar 4: accent on and-of-2. The groove walks forward through time.
Root 2: filter opens on whatever beat the accent lands — it walks WITH the accent.
Secondary: resonance peaks where accent WAS last bar (echo of previous position). Creates a "shadow" groove.
Tertiary: micro-movement biased toward the current accent position — the whole sound leans where the groove is.
06 — Ricochet
Call and response between two rhythmic patterns. Root 1 hits bars 1+3, Root 2 answers bars 2+4.
Root 1: rhythmic pattern A (call) Root 2: rhythmic pattern B (response)
Root 1: sharp staccato hits — 3 quick stabs at start of bar 1 and 3, then silence. Short, aggressive.
Root 2: rolls/fills that respond in bar 2 and 4 — longer, more fluid. Like a drummer responding to a horn stab.
Secondary: reverb only on the response (bar 2+4). Drive only on the call (bar 1+3). Each side has its own character.
Tertiary: lanes split in half — odd lanes follow call energy, even lanes follow response energy. Two groove armies.
07 — Wobble
LFO that speeds up and slows down. Not a fixed rate — the wobble breathes.
Root 1: variable-rate sine wobble Root 2: wobble rate envelope
Root 1: sine wave but the frequency changes: bar 1 = slow (1Hz), bar 2 = faster (4Hz), bar 3 = peak speed (8Hz), bar 4 = decelerates back to 2Hz. The wobble itself tells a story.
Root 2: represents the rate — a slow arc that controls how fast Root 1 oscillates.
Secondary: resonance peaks when wobble is slow (more audible), ducks when fast (prevents screech). Drive increases with speed.
Tertiary: phase-cascaded wobble — each lane at the same variable rate but offset. 30 wobbles slightly out of phase = massive movement.
08 — Stagger
4 rhythmic hits per bar, each one different. No two bars feel the same. Constantly evolving groove.
Root 1: 4 unique hits per bar, changing each bar
Root 1: bar 1: short-short-long-short. Bar 2: long-short-short-long. Bar 3: short-long-short-rest. Bar 4: short-short-short-long-long (5 against 4 feel). Each hit has different value (0.5-1.0) and different decay time.
Secondary: each hit type triggers a different secondary behavior. Short hits → drive spike. Long hits → filter sweep. Rests → delay bloom.
Tertiary: follow the rhythm at micro level but with randomized intensity per hit — no two tertiary lanes accent the same hit.
09 — Pendulum
Swinging motion. Not a sine — more like a pendulum with gravity. Faster at the bottom, lingers at the top.
Root 1: pendulum curve (fast bottom, slow top) Root 2: swing period changes
Root 1: pendulum arc — spends more time at peaks (lingers at 0 and 1), rushes through the middle. Bar 1-2: wide swing (0→1→0→1). Bar 3-4: swing narrows (0.3→0.7→0.3→0.7) — energy winding down.
Root 2: the swing gets shorter over 4 bars — losing momentum.
Secondary: resonance peaks at the "linger" moments (top of swing). Drive peaks at the "rush" moments (middle). They alternate.
Tertiary: each lane is a slightly smaller pendulum — like harmonics of the main swing. Lane 50 barely moves. Lane 2 swings nearly as wide as root.
10 — Fracture
Starts whole, breaks apart. Bar 1 is a clean groove, each bar removes/scrambles pieces until bar 4 is fragments.
Root 1: groove that degrades Root 2: fragmentation control
Root 1: bar 1: clean 4/4 pump. Bar 2: beat 3 splits into two shorter hits. Bar 3: beats get shuffled, gaps appear. Bar 4: stutter fragments — 32nd note shards with silence between.
Root 2: ramp 0→1 representing "how broken" the groove is. Controls how derived lanes respond.
Secondary: as fragmentation rises — reverb increases (fills the gaps), drive increases (shards get harsher), filter narrows (focus on mid).
Tertiary: lanes fragment at different rates. Some break early (bar 2), some hold together until bar 4. Creates a dissolving effect across the whole rack.

Pumper Variants 6 presets

11 — The Pumper (Tight)
The classic evolving pump from the explorations. Tight→syncopated→open→double-time.
See exploration #01. 4 root lanes. Secondary reactive per zone. The reference preset.
12 — The Pumper (Half-Time)
2-bar pump cycle. Hit + long decay. Secondary fills the decay zone.
See exploration #10. Spacious, heavy, hypnotic. Resonance and delay bloom during the long tail.
13 — The Pumper (Reverse)
Inverted pump — values rise BEFORE the beat, snap down ON the beat. Reverse sidechain feel.
Root 1: reverse pump — slow ramp 0→1 over each beat, then instant snap to 0 on the next beat. Creates a "sucking in" feel instead of "pushing out".
All derivation is the same reactive system, but the energy flows backward.
14 — The Pumper (Triplet)
3-against-4 pump feel. Creates polyrhythmic tension against a straight beat.
Root 1: pump on dotted-8th grid (triplet feel). 3 pumps per bar instead of 4. Bar 3-4: switches to straight 4 — the moment the polyrhythm resolves is the climax.
Secondary: reacts to the grid switch — filter opens wider when the rhythm is "wrong" (triplet zone), tightens when it resolves.
15 — The Pumper (Breathing)
Pump depth oscillates — bars 1+3 pump hard, bars 2+4 pump gently. Inhale/exhale on the groove itself.
Root 1: pump pattern where the DEPTH changes: bar 1 pumps 0→1, bar 2 pumps 0.3→0.7, bar 3 back to 0→1, bar 4 pumps 0.4→0.6. The groove breathes in intensity.
16 — The Pumper (Decay)
Fixed pump attack, but the decay shape changes each bar — sharp, medium, long, exponential.
Root 1: all 4 bars have the same attack (instant snap to 1). But: bar 1 = linear decay. Bar 2 = convex decay (holds high then drops). Bar 3 = concave decay (drops then holds low). Bar 4 = exponential (fast start, long tail). Same rhythm, 4 different textures.

Sweep & Evolve 5 presets

17 — Slow Burn
Glacial WT sweep. Filter fills the silence, goes quiet when WT takes over.
See exploration #02. 8-bar version. Secondary lanes trade off ownership of movement.
18 — Deep Breath
Inhale/exhale. Reverb blooms on inhale only. Drive compresses on exhale only.
See exploration #06. Each phase has different secondary owners.
19 — Tide
Two slow sweeps in opposite directions crossing in the middle. The intersection is where magic happens.
Root 1: sweep up 0→1 over 4 bars. Root 2: sweep down 1→0 over 4 bars. They cross at bar 2. Secondary lanes react to the DISTANCE between the two roots — when they're far apart (bar 1, 4) lanes are calm. When they cross (bar 2) all secondary lanes peak with activity. The crossing point is the climax.
20 — Orbit
Two sine waves at different rates creating a Lissajous-like pattern. Complex but smooth.
Root 1: sine at 1 cycle per 4 bars. Root 2: sine at 3 cycles per 4 bars (3:1 ratio). The phase relationship creates a complex evolving pattern that takes 4 bars to repeat. Secondary lanes track the combined energy (sum of both roots). Tertiary phase-cascade from the combined shape.
21 — Glacier
Almost imperceptible movement. 8-bar sweep at 3% range. The subtlest preset — you feel the change, never hear it.
Root 1: sweep 0.48→0.52 over 8 bars. That's it. 4% range. All derived lanes: micro-drift at 2-5% intensity. The entire preset is a whisper. Designed to add "life" to a completely static sound without any obvious modulation.

Build & Surge 4 presets

22 — Surge
Staircase build with fake drop at 75%. Real drop at end. Cascading release.
See exploration #03. Reverb goes huge at fake drop, snaps dry at real drop.
23 — Ignite
Exponential build — slow start, then accelerates violently into the peak. No fake drop, just raw energy.
Root 1: exponential curve — barely moves bar 1, noticeable bar 2, fast bar 3, vertical bar 4. Secondary: each secondary lane has a different "ignition point" — some start moving in bar 2, others wait until bar 3, a few only activate in bar 4. Creates a cascade of lanes joining the build. Like an engine starting.
24 — Collapse
Reverse of Surge — starts at full intensity, deconstructs over 4 bars. Everything falls apart beautifully.
Root 1: starts at 1.0, staircase DOWN. Each step drops lower. Secondary: as root drops, reverb grows (space fills the emptiness), drive drops (loses edge), resonance drifts up (gets more hollow). Tertiary: lanes drop out one by one — by bar 4, only a few whispers remain.
25 — Tension
No release. Just builds and builds. 4 bars of increasing everything. Designed to pair before a drop someone else writes.
Root 1: linear ramp 0→0.9 (never reaches 1 — unresolved). Root 2: noise/activity increases alongside. Secondary: every lane rises but at different rates. Filter fast, reverb slow, drive medium. Tertiary: density of micro-movement increases bar by bar. Bar 4 feels like it's about to explode but doesn't.

Texture & Ghost 3 presets

26 — Ghost Machine
Invisible modulation. 30+ lanes at 5-15%. Nothing audible alone, everything alive together.
See exploration #04. Different drift rates per param type. Correlated counter-motion between clusters.
27 — Shimmer
Like Ghost Machine but biased upward — micro-movements in the 0.7-1.0 range. Adds sparkle to bright sounds.
Same drift system as Ghost Machine but all values oscillate around 0.85 instead of 0.5. Designed for high-frequency content — makes bright pads and leads glisten without obvious modulation.
28 — Murk
Like Ghost Machine biased downward — micro-movements in 0.0-0.3 range. Adds darkness and depth to low sounds.
Drift around 0.15. Designed for bass, dark pads, sub textures. The subtle movement adds life without brightening. Filter lanes drift near their minimum, keeping things dark but alive.

Cascade & Special 2 presets

29 — Cascade
One curve ripples across 100 lanes. Amplitude and shape mutate as the wave travels.
See exploration #08. First lanes full intensity, last lanes ghost whispers. The wave shape softens as it spreads.
30 — Chaotic Engine
Alternating chaos and clarity. Reverb is the inverse anchor — huge in calm moments, dry in madness.
See exploration #07. Rules deliberately broken in chaos zones. The structure is the alternation itself.
