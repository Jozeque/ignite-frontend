Compact toolbar, grouped

The real patched #sd-compact-toolbar from the shipping wrapper, the bar the VST actually shows. Group titles sit inline at the head of each family, so this costs zero extra height. Nothing was moved, renamed or rewired: 39 buttons in, 39 out.

Families: Draw, Shape, Vary and Selected lanes on the first row. Motion, Bars and Saved on the second, with the bulk lane actions pushed right. Selected lanes is the per-lane generator set, named for its scope so it reads against Motion, which hits every mapped lane.
