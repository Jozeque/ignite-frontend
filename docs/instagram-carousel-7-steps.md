# Stride — Instagram Carousel: 7 Steps

**Format:** 1080×1350 px (4:5 portrait — Instagram's highest-engagement aspect ratio for carousels)
**Total slides:** 10 (cover + 7 steps + payoff + CTA)
**Tone:** confident, simple, anti-overwhelming. Make it look easy.
**Font:** Outfit (300/400/500/700/900) — matches the landing page

## Visual identity (locked)

- **Background:** `#09090b` (dark base)
- **Action text (largest):** white, Outfit Black 900, ~80–120px depending on slide
- **Explainer text (medium):** zinc-300, Outfit Medium 500, ~22–24px
- **Tiny gotcha text:** zinc-400 base, with color-coded prefix:
  - **amber-400** for "could go wrong" notes (preceded by ⚠)
  - **red-400** for "most-likely-mistake" notes (preceded by ⚠)
  - **zinc-500** for neutral hints
- **Step number badge:** orange/red gradient, large
- **Whitespace:** lots. Sparse > full.

## Design rules (locked)

1. **One idea per slide.** Don't cram two steps onto one slide.
2. **Three text sizes max:** HUGE (action), medium (the how), tiny (the gotcha). No fourth size.
3. **Color hierarchy:**
   - White = the action
   - Zinc-300/400 = the explanation
   - Amber-400 = caution / could go wrong
   - Red-400 = the most likely mistake
   - Orange/red gradient = brand + step numbers
4. **Lots of whitespace.** The slide should feel sparse. Better to leave a third of the slide empty than fill it.
5. **No more than 3 lines of copy at any size.** Split the slide if needed.
6. **Visual = supporting, not central.** Small inline visuals or icons, not full-page screenshots.

## Production notes for designer (you, or whoever's making it in Canva/Figma)

- Use Outfit weight 900 for ALL big "action" text — bold and confident
- Use Outfit weight 500 for explainer text — readable, not too heavy
- Use Outfit weight 700 for the tiny notes — needs to feel important despite being small
- Step number badge: 80px circle/rounded-square with orange→red gradient, white number inside
- For the gotcha text, use a small ⚠ icon or just the unicode character — adds visual weight without being noisy
- Consider a subtle Stride curve as a background motif on every slide for brand consistency
- Test legibility on a phone screen — what looks fine on desktop might be too small at 6"
- Export each slide as PNG or JPEG, 1080×1350px
- For the carousel post: order matters — IG shows slide 1 in feed; the rest unlock on tap

## Variations to consider for A/B testing

- **Reels version (vertical 1080×1920):** same content, animated transitions between slides, voiceover narrating each step
- **Shorter cut (5 slides):** Steps 1+2 on one slide, 3+4 on another, etc. — for users who skip 10-slide carousels
- **TikTok version:** vertical, 30-60sec, voiceover, screen recording transitions instead of static slides

## When to post

- Pre-launch: "Coming soon — here's how it works" framing
- Launch day: "$39 founding member pricing — first 100"
- Post-launch (3-5 weeks in): testimonials carousel as a follow-up
