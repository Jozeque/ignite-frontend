What changed, and nothing else. movedDevice row now reads left to right as a workflow: browser, mapping, transport, chain. + Add sits with Favorites because it loads a device, it does not map one.
movedTool row is reordered into edit mode, variation, selected-lane motion, character, then Swing. Undo and Redo lose the prime left edge and sit far right.
demotedMirror, Flip, 2x, 1/2x to the lane right-click. Clear to the chain context menu. Chip open and close, and the lane remove, to hover.
cutnone pinned filler, and the two global synth-window icons. Save folds into Motions.
keptEvery generator, every character tool, every Bars value. Nothing creative was hidden to make the screenshot tidier.
The spacing rule doing the work. 4px inside a family, 11px between families, and a 1px divider with 8px either side between different scopes. That is the whole mechanism. Compare the two silhouettes with your eyes half closed: today is one long dashed line, the proposal is five distinct masses per row.
