How Stride VST Works

Build your dream chain in one place, modulate everything you map at once, and get endless unique variations in a few clicks.

Click anything on the plugin and see exactly what it does. Fully interactive, exactly like the real window.

Before you modulate
  1. Add your devices. Pick a synth and effects from your favorites: Stride hosts the whole chain inside itself.
  2. Map by touch. Arm Map, then click the knobs on the synth you want to move. Each one drops onto the canvas as a lane.
  3. Or route audio in. New in 1.2: send a guitar, vocal, or hardware track into Stride, build an FX chain, and everything you map moves while you play.
