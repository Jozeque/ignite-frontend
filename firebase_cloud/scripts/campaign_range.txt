Subject: New in Stride: Range

Hi{name_part},

Quick update, and it's all about control.

You can now set a Range on any parameter. Give a lane a min and a max, and Stride keeps every curve inside those bounds. Sweep a filter between 30 and 60 percent instead of the whole way. Decide exactly how far each parameter is allowed to move, and keep the motion musical.

Also new: Unmap. Pull any parameter out of Stride's control with a single click, no rescanning, no rebuilding your map.

Replace your Stride VST:
1. Re-download Stride from your original Lemon Squeezy receipt.
2. Drop the new Stride.vst3 into your VST3 folder, replacing the old file.
   Windows: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   Mac: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. Fully quit and reopen your DAW.

Your projects, license, and saved favorites all carry over. Nothing to re-activate.
