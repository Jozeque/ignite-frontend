Stride is now a VST3. It runs in every DAW, not just Ableton, and it hosts a full chain of your own instruments and effects so you can draw a curve and modulate all of it live.

You were one of the first people to back Stride, so this is my gift to you: the VST is yours, free, as a thank you for being an early adopter. Open your original Stride receipt from Lemon Squeezy and click the same download link you already have. The VST is now right there next to your download, no new purchase.

Setting it up takes about two minutes:

1. From your receipt, download and unzip the Stride VST for your system.
2. Put Stride.vst3 into your VST3 folder.
   Windows: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   Mac: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. Fully quit and reopen your DAW so it rescans plugins (it will not hot-reload).
4. Add Stride to a track and paste the same license key you already use. No new key, no extra step.

Then load a synth, draw a curve, and watch the whole chain move.
