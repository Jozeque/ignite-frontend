TO UPDATE (about 3 min)
1. Open your original Lemon Squeezy purchase email and hit Download. Same link, it serves the new build. No re-purchase.
2. Quit Ableton and Stride.
3. Replace your Stride folder (extract the new zip over the old).
4. Open the new Stride, let it activate. Your license carries over.
5. Click "Install to Ableton" in the toolbar.
6. In Ableton: right-click StrideLink, Delete, then drag a fresh one from User Library > Stride. (Ableton holds the old device in memory, so this step matters.)

If Inject to Clip doesn't fire right after updating, open Settings > Link, Tempo & MIDI, set Control Surface to None, then pick StrideInject again. That reloads the new inject engine.

Your saves, sessions, and license all carry through. Reply if anything acts up.
