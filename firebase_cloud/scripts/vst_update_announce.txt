A new Stride VST update just dropped. Here is what is new:

1. Modulate only what you select. There is a new 'sel' section that applies motion to just the parameters you pick. The Motion buttons still hit everything, so you get both.

2. Five new Neuro variants. The 'sel' section just gained five fresh Neuro flavors to throw at your selected parameters.

{{img:https://storage.googleapis.com/veero-next.firebasestorage.app/email-assets/stride-update-sel.png}}

3. Print your modulation into your DAW. A new button sends Stride's movement to your track as real automation lanes. While Stride is playing, the track follows Stride live. When your DAW plays, Stride steps aside so you can shape those lanes yourself. (In Ableton, click 'Configure' on the Stride device first so it copies the parameters over.)

{{img:https://storage.googleapis.com/veero-next.firebasestorage.app/email-assets/stride-update-senddaw.png}}

4. Reorder your chain by dragging. Drop one device onto another to swap their positions.

5. Load Kontakt into Stride and modulate everything inside it.

Updating takes a minute:

1. Re-download Stride from your original Lemon Squeezy receipt.
2. Unzip it and drop Stride.vst3 into your VST3 folder, replacing the old file.
   Windows: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   Mac: /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. Fully quit and reopen your DAW so it rescans.

Your projects, your license, and your saved favorites all carry over. Nothing to re-activate, nothing to set up again.
