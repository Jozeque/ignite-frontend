Built from the sound up

Every StrideChain is a sound design system.

Not a preset with a few knobs on a lane. Each chain was built as one piece: the patch, the lanes that move it, the range of every lane, the knobs that stay locked and five states that each take the sound somewhere new. Hundreds of decisions per chain, made so the only thing left for you is to explore.

How it works

From the inbox to the first sound.

1

Get the Collection, or your packs

The download links come to your inbox right after checkout.

2

Put them in your chains folder

Unzip into Documents\Stride\chains (Mac: ~/Documents/Stride/chains).

Load, pick a state, press play

Then make it yours: redraw a lane, reroll it, lock what you love.

Questions

Before you pick.

Do I need the synths?

Yes. Each pack plays the synth it was built on: Serum 2, Vital, Diva or Tendril. Synths are not included. Take only the packs for the synths you have, or the whole Collection.

Do I need Stride?

Yes. StrideChains open in Stride, in every DAW, and Stride is what moves the racks.

Do I need Ableton Live?

Only for the racks. The sounds are samples for any DAW, and the StrideChains open in Stride wherever you run it.

I already have the Experimental Collection.

Then its sounds and racks are already yours. Take the StrideChains packs on their own.

What is a state?

A full take on the chain: the patch, the lanes, their ranges and their shapes. Five in each chain, five different directions from the same sound, one click apart.

What are Stride wavetables?

Wavetables built at Stride from scratch: reese basses, talking mouths, bowed strings, struck glass, bytebeat programs and the Experimental Collection's own samples pressed into tables. 26 of the 40 Serum 2 chains and all 40 Vital chains run on them and carry them inside, so there is nothing to install. The other 14 run on Serum 2's own granular and spectral engines. Those two packs also bring the whole bank of 111, ready for Serum 2's Tables folder or Vital's Import Bank.

Which versions?

Built on Serum 2.0.23, Vital 1.6.4, Diva 1.4.5 and Tendril 3.1.0.

Can I change them?

That is the point. Redraw any lane, move its range, unlock a knob, save your own states. The chain is yours to take apart.

Every chain is a place to start. Where it goes is yours.
