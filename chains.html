A chain is a whole setup saved in one file. Load it and everything comes back: the devices, every mapped knob, the ranges you are allowed to move inside, and the curves already drawn. Hit a generator and it is your sound from there.

Two kinds of download

.stridechain

The chain file

Stride's own format. It carries the mappings, the ranges, the lane speeds, the loop lengths and every curve. Open Stride, hit Load, pick the file.

Smallest download, and the one to grab when you already own the devices it uses and want the motion rather than the patch.

.adg · Ableton rack

The full rack

An Ableton Instrument Rack with Stride and the devices inside it, wired up. The routing is done, the ranges are set. Drop it on a track and play.

Bigger, and Ableton only, but nothing to reconnect. This is the one to grab if you want it working in one drag.

  1. 01Drop the file anywhere in your Ableton User Library.

  3. 03Everything is already wired. Play.

A good home for it is User Library / Presets / Instruments / Instrument Rack.

Before you download

A chain points at real devices, so you need the ones it lists. Every card says what it wants. If a plugin is missing, Stride loads the rest of the chain and leaves that lane empty rather than refusing the file.

Chains that drive Ableton's own devices through StrideBridge are tied to where those devices sat when the chain was saved. Open Map Live and click the device header to point a lane at your own. The rack downloads carry their devices with them, so there is nothing to reconnect.
