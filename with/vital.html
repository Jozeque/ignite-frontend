How it works

From your patch to a new take in three steps.

  1. 01

    Load your favorite tools

    Load Vital and the effects you want around it inside Stride. VST3 on Windows and macOS, AU on macOS.

  2. 02

    Map the knobs

    Press MAP and touch the controls you want to move. Each one becomes a lane on the canvas, with its own range.

  3. 03

    Press, lock, regenerate, and edit

    Press Chaos, Neuro or S&H and every unlocked lane gets new motion, up to 32 bars. Keep what works, regenerate the rest, edit any curve by hand.
