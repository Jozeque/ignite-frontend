A two-hour conversation about happy accidents

Derek Oswald from AltWire sat with us for two hours on the happy accidents inside a sound design session, then spent real time with Stride and wrote a deep dive.

September 20, 2026·1 min read

We spent nearly two hours on Zoom with Derek Oswald from AltWire, talking about much more than features and buttons.

We talked about the happy accidents that happen inside sound design sessions. The moments you could never really plan for, but somehow end up defining a track. About how every producer develops their own musical and sonic fingerprint over time, not only through the tools they use, but through the way they explore them.

A big part of Stride came from thinking about those moments. How do you create more opportunities to stumble into something unexpected, without giving up control? How do you explore more of what is already sitting inside your instruments and effects, instead of repeating the same familiar moves?

Derek took that conversation, spent real time with Stride, and turned it into an incredibly thoughtful deep dive into the ideas behind it and the workflow itself.
