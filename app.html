Engine Manual

Parameter Breakdown

STUDIO ASSISTANT: Talk to the Engine naturally. It determines key, BPM, and style entirely based on your words.

THE RHYTHM ENGINE: Dedicated drum groove generator. Sets specific velocity rules and mathematical swing mapping perfectly suited for drum racks.

THE SEED ENGINE: Drag any MIDI file. The engine parses it on the fly and generates basslines, counter-melodies, or chords synced perfectly to your seed without altering the original.

COSMIC MODE: Dreamy, cinematic sequence generation. Uses strict rhythmic algorithms (Fibonacci, Tesla 3-6-9) and applies ancient Just Intonation tuning via sequential pitchwheel micro-bending. No block chords.

Cosmic Flow: Controls the density and speed of the sequence. <40% = Nebula (Sparse/Dreamy), 40-70% = Orbit (Pulsing), >70% = Quantum (Rolling/Fast).

Glissando (Micro-Strum): Spawns rapid 3 to 6-note cascades. The engine enforces an absolute 25ms physical delay between notes to sound human.

Humanize / Swing: Shifts notes slightly off-grid to create a natural, human micro-groove.

Voicing Mode: Switch between MONO (single notes, ideal for leads/bass) and POLY (chords).

Harmonic Density: Scales complexity from simple dyads (Lvl 2) to lush, complex jazz chords (Lvl 5).
