Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026  |  Effective: April 1, 2026  |  Version 1.0

1. Who We Are

Stride ("we", "us", "our") operates the web application at stridehub.io. Stride is a sound design engine for Ableton Live that helps electronic music producers generate automation curves and MIDI patterns for their instrument racks.

For privacy inquiries, contact us at: home@stridehub.io

2. What Data We Collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Email address (at sign-up)
  • Artist / display name (at sign-up)
  • Password (hashed — we never see or store your plaintext password)
  • Ableton instrument rack files (.alc) you upload for processing
  • MIDI files you upload for processing
  • Text prompts and generation preferences you submit
  • Google account information if you use Google Sign-In (name, email)

Data we collect automatically:

  • Generation history (timestamps, settings used, file references)
  • Credit usage and account activity
  • Account metadata (creation date, last active date)

Data we do NOT collect:

  • We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics
  • We do not collect browsing behavior, IP addresses for profiling, or device fingerprints
  • We do not purchase data from third parties

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the service: process your racks and generate automations, MIDI, and sound design outputs
  • Account management: authentication, credit tracking, generation history
  • AI processing: your rack parameters and generation settings are sent to Google Gemini API to generate outputs (see Section 5)
  • Service communications: account verification, critical service updates

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing without your explicit consent.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract performance: account data, service delivery, file processing — necessary to provide the service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interest: usage tracking for credit management, security monitoring, service improvement
  • Consent: marketing communications (if any), processing of data via Google Sign-In

5. AI Processing

Stride uses Google's Gemini API (paid tier) to process your generation requests. When you generate, the following data is sent to Google Gemini:

  • Rack parameter names and value ranges from your uploaded .alc files
  • MIDI note data (if provided)
  • Your generation settings (style, key, BPM, bars, prompt text)

Important: We use the paid Gemini API tier. Under Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum, your data is not used to train Google's AI models. Google processes data solely to deliver the API response and does not retain it beyond the processing window.

Generated outputs (MIDI files, automation data, .alc files) are yours. Stride does not claim any ownership over content generated through the service.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where Google, Supabase, and Lemon Squeezy operate servers. These transfers are protected by:

  • The EU-US Data Privacy Framework
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable

Google, Supabase, and Lemon Squeezy are certified under applicable international transfer mechanisms.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Generation history: retained while your account is active
  • Generated files (MIDI, ALC): retained until you delete them or your account
  • Billing records: retained as required by applicable tax law (up to 7 years)

Upon account deletion, all personal data is purged from our systems within 30 days.

9. Your Rights

All users:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export — request your data in a machine-readable format (JSON)

Additional rights for EU/UK residents (GDPR):

  • Restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Object to processing — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw at any time
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local Data Protection Authority

To exercise any of these rights, email home@stridehub.io. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Data Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • All data is encrypted at rest (Firebase and Supabase default encryption)
  • Passwords are hashed by Firebase Authentication — we never store plaintext passwords
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel only

11. Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to maintain your authentication session. This is strictly necessary for the service to function — it keeps you logged in between visits.

We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. No third-party tracking technologies are present on our site.

12. Children's Privacy

Stride is not directed at children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will delete it promptly.

Previous versions are available upon request.
