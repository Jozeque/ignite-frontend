Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026  |  Effective: September 22, 2026  |  Version 1.3

1. Who We Are

Stride ("we", "us", "our") publishes the Stride desktop application and operates the website at stridehub.io. Stride is a sound design engine. It runs on your own machine as a VST3 plugin in any VST3 host, and as Max for Live components in Ableton Live, and it helps electronic music producers design modulation curves and apply them to the instruments, effects and Ableton devices in their own setup.

For privacy inquiries, contact us at: home@stridehub.io

2. What Data We Collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Email address (when you sign up to the waitlist or purchase a license)
  • Artist / display name (if you provide one at waitlist signup — optional)
  • Payment and billing information, collected by our payment provider, see Section 6

Data we collect automatically:

  • License key status (validated against our backend when you activate or re-check)
  • Approximate activation machine identifier (hostname) — so your license can be tied to the devices you installed it on

Data we do NOT collect:

  • Your project and rack files, presets, MIDI clips, automation curves, or any content you create in Stride: all of that stays on your local machine and is never transmitted to us
  • We do not collect device fingerprints
  • We do not purchase data from third parties

Our website uses the Meta Pixel and Google Ads conversion tracking to measure ad-campaign performance, and Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use the site. See Section 6 (Third Parties) and Section 11 (Local Storage) for details and your opt-out options.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver the service: validate your license key when you activate Stride or launch it periodically
  • Order fulfillment: deliver your license key and receipt to the email you provided at purchase
  • Service communications: critical updates (e.g. a bug-fix release, security advisory) sent to the email you used for purchase or waitlist signup

We use the Meta Pixel on our landing page and post-purchase confirmation page, and Google Ads conversion tracking on our website and from our order records, to measure ad-campaign performance. See Section 6 for details. Aside from those, we do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or marketing without your explicit consent.

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity on our website to understand how visitors use it (the pages they visit, clicks, scrolling, heatmaps and session replays), so we can improve the site. Text you type into form fields is masked by Clarity before it leaves your browser. See Section 6.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract performance: order processing, license delivery, license validation — necessary to provide the product you purchased
  • Legitimate interest: limiting license activations per seat, preventing fraud, critical service notices
  • Consent: marketing communications, if any

5. Local-First Processing

Stride runs on your own computer, as a plugin inside your DAW and as Max for Live components inside Ableton Live. All sound design work happens entirely on your local machine: scanning the devices you host or map, drawing automation curves, and writing the motion back into your session. Your project files, MIDI data, instrument presets, and canvas state never leave your computer.

The only network traffic Stride initiates is:

  • An occasional license-key validation call to our backend (Cloud Functions on Firebase), which checks your key against our own license store and, for older orders, Lemon Squeezy
  • Downloading application updates, when prompted

Whatever you create in Stride is yours. Stride does not claim any ownership over content you produce with the software.

11. Local Storage & Cookies

Stride's desktop app stores your license status, settings, and your canvas sessions locally on your computer (in your user-data folder). This data is never transmitted to us and is purely there to keep the app working between launches.

Our website uses the Meta Pixel on our landing page and on the post-purchase confirmation page to measure ad-campaign performance (see Section 6). The pixel sets a _fbp first-party cookie used by Meta to attribute ad clicks to site visits and purchases. The website also uses Google Analytics, which sets _ga cookies, and Microsoft Clarity, which sets _clck and _clsk cookies and may set Microsoft cookies such as MUID, to measure how visitors use the site. For visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Clarity sets no cookies unless consent is given. Google Ads conversion tracking sets _gcl_au and, after you arrive from one of our ads, _gcl_aw first-party cookies, which record the click that brought you here so a later demo registration or purchase can be attributed to that ad. We do not use any other cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. To opt out, adjust your Facebook ad preferences or your Google ad settings (see Section 6) or use a tracker-blocking browser extension.
