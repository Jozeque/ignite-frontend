A note before you start

Stride is a sound design tool, not a mixing tool. You'll draw curves first, then drag the .alc into Ableton to hear them. That loop is built for creative exploration — picking an Ableton rack (synth + effects), scanning every parameter, designing curves you'd never draw by hand. Lean into it for variation, motion, and "what does this rack do that I haven't tried yet" — not for surgical mix work.
