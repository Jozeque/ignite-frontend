FAQ

The questions everyone asks

What do I get for $79?▾
Everything - both editions in one purchase. Stride VST for every DAW (host your synths + effects and modulate them), and StrideLink for Ableton (modulate your racks and native devices, inject motion into clips). One price, no add-ons.
Which DAWs does Stride VST work in?▾
Any VST3 host: Ableton Live, Cubase, Nuendo, Studio One, FL Studio, Reaper, Bitwig, Cakewalk and more. Not Logic (AU only) or Pro Tools (AAX only).
Can Stride VST modulate my other plugins or native devices?▾
It modulates any VST3 instrument or effect you load inside Stride VST. It can't reach devices sitting next to it on the track - that's a VST3 rule, not a Stride limit. If you're on Ableton and want to modulate native devices in place, that's StrideLink.
Can't I just draw automation by hand?▾
You can, and Stride never takes that away: every lane stays editable, point by point, right on the canvas. What changes is scale. By hand, motion across a whole rack means one lane at a time. Stride writes all of them in one click, then hands you a fresh take on the next one. You shape, lock, or redraw whatever you want. Final say is always yours.
How is this different from an LFO tool?▾
LFO tools are built around a shape that repeats. Stride writes long-form evolving motion, up to 32 bars of it, across every lane of the chain you host inside it, and rolls a brand-new variation every time you click. Not a cycle you set: a performance you discover.
Can I play it live?▾
Yes. Keyswitch Mode fires the Motion tools from one octave of your keyboard: the notes stay silent, the tools fire. Note-triggered motion runs the canvas from the notes you play, no transport needed. Keys fire the tools, notes drive the motion, and both record into a clip.
