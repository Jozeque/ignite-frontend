2. What Stride Is · System Requirements

Stride is a sound design engine for Ableton Live. It consists of a desktop application (Windows and macOS) and a Max for Live device (StrideLink) that work together to turn your existing instrument racks into modulation playgrounds. Stride generates automation curves and Ableton clip files (.alc) which you drag into your Live set.

You must have all of the following to use Stride. It is your responsibility to verify this before purchase:

  • Ableton Live 11 Suite or higher — OR Ableton Live 11 Standard plus the Max for Live add-on. Stride does NOT work with Ableton Live Lite, Intro, or versions older than 11.
  • Python 3 installed on your computer (pre-installed on macOS; Windows users can install it from python.org).
  • macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later, or Windows 10/11.
  • An internet connection for initial license activation.

Stride does NOT replace Ableton Live, does NOT generate audio, and cannot be used as a standalone music production tool. It works ALONGSIDE Ableton to automate instrument rack parameters.
